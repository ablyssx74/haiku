# snaketracker 1.0.51

Tracker, Deskbar and menus with the hDesktop "snake trail" look.

## What's new
- **The Deskbar's own window has rounded corners.** Every corner of the bar that is not a corner of the screen is round, in all six places the Deskbar can sit (top, bottom, and the four corners, expanded or not). The window system's square border is gone from the Deskbar: its window is made without one, and thin lines along the sides draw the outline in its place, round at the corners, and along the sides at the edge of the screen too. A small picture of the screen behind each corner is taken while the bar is hidden for a moment (a fraction of a second, when the bar starts or its size or place changes; it can blink then), so the wallpaper, or a window, shows through the corners. With **Enable rounded corners** off the corners are square, with the same outline. It follows the Raised Snake look and rounded corners settings.
- **The program tabs of a vertical Deskbar run to the edge of the window.** The header of each program, the dark box under it with its windows, and the row you point at are straight at the end that faces the edge of the screen and round at the other; with the Deskbar on the left they are the other way round. The tab has a soft shade along its bottom like the leaf's block, and its chevron is a darker shade of the tab's color. A horizontal Deskbar keeps its pill tabs.
- **The window menus of a Deskbar at the bottom of the screen are in the snake look.** A menu that opens upward from a tab (the list of a program's windows, and "No windows") had the picture of the screen behind it taken for a menu that opens downward: a tall menu showed the window system's square frame along its top, and a short one was drawn in the stock look. The picture is taken in the right place now.
- **The SnakeTracker pane of Tracker preferences says more.** The options are named **Raised Snake look for Tracker and Deskbar**, **Enable rounded corners on Tracker & Deskbar** and **Snake look in all programs & menus**; the **[?]** help of the first, the rounded corners and the all programs options is rewritten, and the rounded corners option is greyed out while the Raised Snake look is off (it keeps its value for when the look is back on).

Since 1.0.50 (if you skipped it):
- **Rounded corners are on by default, and the menus really look see-through.** The thin dark square frame the window system draws round a menu is no longer visible: it is covered with the part of the screen behind the menu (four borderless one-pixel windows over its sides), so the rounded corners show the wallpaper, or the window, behind them. It follows the menu as it scrolls and as submenus open and close; where a submenu or the Deskbar joins a menu, the strips stop short and the seam is left as it was. A menu as tall as the screen is handled too. If you had turned **Enable rounded corners on Tracker** off, it stays off.
- **The menus in a Tracker window's menu bar have the snake look with rounded corners.** File, Window and the others (and their submenus) were drawn in Haiku's stock look with raised trails when rounded corners were on; they now have the accent color, the raised look and the rounded corners like every other Tracker menu.
- **The Deskbar's menus have rounded corners too, with no frame.** A menu that hangs flush from the Deskbar's bar (a horizontal Deskbar at the top or bottom of the screen) is square at its far corners, so it no longer shows the bar's own border line behind a rounded corner.
- **Your Tracker settings survive a restart of the desktop.** When Haiku's own Tracker was stopped to make way for SnakeTracker's (at start-up, and on every restart by name), it saved the settings file with only the settings it knows, and all the SnakeTracker settings, and Hide Desktop Icons, were lost. The server now keeps the file while it swaps them and puts it back.
- **Small fixes.** The black corner pixel where a submenu's border meets its parent's is covered. A long run of browsing through deeply nested folders no longer throws away the picture of the first menu (which, once it was gone, made menus fall back to the stock look).

Since 1.0.49 (if you skipped it):
- **Hide Desktop Icons.** A new item in the Desktop's menu, under "Mini icon view" (shortcut Alt+3), hides every icon on the Desktop and puts a check mark on the item; choose it again to bring them back. The wallpaper and the Desktop's menu stay as they are. While the icons are hidden they cannot be clicked, selected (with the pointer or Select all) or reached with the arrow keys, so a hidden file can't be acted on by accident, and each icon keeps its place for when they come back. The choice is kept in Tracker's settings (`HideDesktopIcons`), so it holds after a restart. A file you create on the Desktop while the icons are hidden stays out of sight until they are shown again.
- **Arrange the Desktop's icons in columns or in rows.** A new **Arrange** item in the Desktop's menu, under Hide Desktop Icons, opens **In Columns** and **In Rows**. In Columns puts the icons down the left side, one column after another; In Rows puts them across the top, one row after another. They go in the order the Desktop is sorted in, starting at the top left, and, while hDesktop is running, a cell and a half is left clear along the bottom edge, where its dock would cover the labels (without hDesktop the icons use the whole height). It happens once, when you choose it: nothing arranges the icons again by itself, and a new file or folder is placed where it was before. The icons keep their places, so the arrangement is there after a restart. The two items are greyed out while the icons are hidden.

Since 1.0.48 (if you skipped it):
- **SnakeTracker tells you when there is a newer version.** About half a minute after the desktop starts, the SnakeTracker server looks at the version number in the repository on GitHub; if it is newer than the one installed, a window says so and offers to open the GitHub page to download it. It does nothing when there is no connection. To turn it off, create an empty file `~/config/settings/snaketracker_no_update_check`. The package now needs the `curl` package (the check is done with libcurl). The version number comes from the new `VERSION` file, which the build also takes the package's version from.
- **Restarting Tracker or Deskbar brings back SnakeTracker.** ProcessController's Restart items, and the Deskbar when it starts Tracker again, ask the system for "the" Tracker by its name, and what the system started could be Haiku's own Tracker or Deskbar (the launch daemon may still have the system's definition, and the two had the same version). The SnakeTracker server now watches for that: when Haiku's own Tracker or Deskbar is started, it is replaced by SnakeTracker's. The two are also numbered one step above Haiku's, so a lookup by name prefers them.
- **ProcessController's menus and hDesktop's CPU graph menu are in the SnakeTracker color again.** Since 1.0.44 they were drawn in Haiku's own colors to keep the trail unbroken; they now have the pill in the SnakeTracker color like every other menu. The columns of numbers in its memory, thread and CPU rows are in the pill's colors too, not boxes of Haiku's own.

Since 1.0.47 (if you skipped it):
- **The separator lines of an add-on's menu items are in the snake look.** An add-on that fills a menu (TrackRunner, for one) adds plain Haiku separators, which showed as the bulky dark stock line. They are replaced by the snake separator, in Tracker's menus and in the right-click popup menus.

Since 1.0.46 (if you skipped it):
- **A menu an add-on puts in a window's menu bar has the snake look.** TrackRunner's menu in a Tracker window's menu bar (and its submenus) was still drawn in the stock look. It is a plain Haiku menu, and Tracker's own menus tell the snake look when they open while a plain one does not, so a plain add-on menu is now replaced by one of Tracker's own, with its items moved over.

Since 1.0.45 (if you skipped it):
- **Menus that a Tracker add-on adds items to keep the snake look.** An add-on that fills a menu (TrackRunner, for one, adds a TrackRunner entry to the File menu and the desktop's menu) adds plain Haiku menu items, and the snake look is given only to a menu whose items it can all draw. Those menus were drawn in the stock look, while their submenus had the snake one. The add-on's items are now replaced by snake ones with the same label, action, shortcut and state, and the add-on's submenus are gone through in turn.

Since 1.0.44 (if you skipped it):
- **The side panel's right-click menu is in the snake look.** The menu that hides the Trash, removes a favorite and the like has the pill shape and colors now.
- **Long menus scroll with less work.** Scrolling a submenu redraws only the rows that changed, about three times less CPU than before.
- **Guards against runaway redraws.** A menu that kept being moved or redrawn could keep a thread busy until the menu closed; this is now rate limited, and the timers that re-checked a menu's frame are no longer left running. To see if a guard ever trips, create an empty file `~/config/settings/snake_debug`; it is then logged to `snake_debug.log` in the same folder.
- **Tracker preferences open again after being closed.** The preferences window was only hidden when closed, so the next open found it hidden and did nothing.
- **ProcessController's menus have an unbroken trail.** Its menus use Haiku's own colors, so the snake trail no longer breaks across its Memory usage row.

Since 1.0.43 (if you skipped it):
- **The menus of every program have the snake look.** Programs that use Haiku's own menus (StyledEdit, Terminal and the like) get the pill look now: a panel in the SnakeTracker color, with the lit row raised and the snake trail joining the submenus. The menus have square corners, and menu bars and programs that draw their own menus (Qt, GTK and others) are not changed. It is on by default; turn it off with **Snake look in all programs** in Tracker preferences > SnakeTracker. A program takes the new look when it is opened.
- **The colors follow the SnakeTracker preferences.** Other programs' menus use the SnakeTracker color, or Haiku's own colors when **Use system colors for Tracker** is on. The text of a stock menu item can't be changed, so the panel suits it: a light tint of the color on a light theme, a dark shade of it on a dark one.
- **The menus of the Deskbar's tray icons have it too.** The popup an icon opens (the network icon, ProcessController, the clock and the like) is drawn in the pill look, without any change to those programs.
- **ProcessController's menus have no arrows.** Every row of them opens a list, and an arrow on each only crowded the usage bars. The rows still open as before.

Since 1.0.42 (if you skipped it):
- **The side panel's selected pill is drawn whole when the window is activated.** Its colour is paler in a window that is not the active one, and only the part of the line that was uncovered was drawn again, which left the end of the pill in the other colour.

Since 1.0.41 (if you skipped it):
- **Submenus open beside their parent, not over it.** They are no longer slid 16 pixels over the parent, which removes most of the artifacts the overlap caused (the stray black vertical line among them). A submenu is lifted a pixel or two so its first row lines up with the parent's, and the parent's black border runs on into the submenu's top and bottom lines. The arrows on the parent's rows that lie beside the open submenu are not drawn (they come back when it closes), so there is no half arrow at its edge.
- **Menus that show their frame occasionally no longer do.** The redraws that were meant to put right a menu whose border strip could not be placed never ran, because they looked for the menu in the wrong place. They run now, a menu is looked at once more a moment after it opens, a hide that arrives late can no longer take down a strip placed for the next menu, and a submenu no longer trusts a parent position saved from an earlier opening.
- **The scroll tabs of long menus are in the snake look.** The top and bottom tabs of a menu taller than the screen are the pill's colour, with an arrow in the trail's colour that dims when there is nothing more to scroll that way.
- **The Attributes menu of Tracker's windows is in the snake look.** It only shows in List view, and it and its file-type submenus were left out of the last update.
- **Rounded corners:** the corner of a menu that faces an open submenu is square where the submenu reaches it, so no backdrop shows between the two; and the mouse pointer no longer leaves a white speck in the corner of a menu (some drivers draw the pointer into the screen, so it was in the captured picture).

Since 1.0.40 (if you skipped it):
- **A SnakeTracker pane in Tracker preferences.** All of SnakeTracker's options moved from the Windows pane to a pane of their own, which has a **[?]** beside each option that explains it when the pointer rests on it. New in it:
  - **Enable rounded corners on Tracker** (off by default). The menus' own corners are square now, so Haiku's black window outline fits round them; with this on they are rounded and the outline shows as a square frame round the menu.
  - **Use system colors for Deskbar** and **Use system colors for Tracker.** On, that program takes the colors of Haiku's Appearance preferences (menu background and selection) in place of the SnakeTracker color.
  - A larger color square, and a **Restart SnakeTracker** button that restarts Tracker and the Deskbar so every setting takes effect.
  - The selected pane in the preferences window's list is a raised pill in the SnakeTracker color.
- **The snake trail stays when the custom look is off.** With **Snake look for Tracker and Deskbar** off, the menus keep Haiku's background and still show the connected trail.
- **Menus are square, with the lit rows and the trail rounded and raised.** The File and Window menus of Tracker's windows have the snake look too.
- **The Deskbar's app list is raised.** Each app's header is a raised pill that fades into the panel like the leaf's block, with a soft shadow under the box of windows, and the tab tool tips are in the pill look. The window menu of an app opens in the snake look also when its windows are listed in the Deskbar.

Since 1.0.39 (if you skipped it):
- **A preference to turn off the custom look of Tracker and the Deskbar.** In Tracker preferences > Windows, **Snake look for Tracker and Deskbar** (on by default). Turn it off and Tracker's and the Deskbar's menus, the Deskbar's panel and the menu bar titles are drawn the way Haiku draws them, while the buttons, scroll bars, check boxes and the rest of the Snake control look stay. hDesktop follows the preference too: with it off, the dock's Tracker menu is the plain menu. The menus change at once; the Deskbar's panel after it is restarted.
- **Right-clicking a folder no longer flashes the stock menu first.** The "Move to Trash" item was a stock item, which made the whole menu ineligible for the pill look until it was drawn again; it is a Snake item now.
- **Big folders are quicker to browse.** A folder with hundreds of entries (`/bin`) made every redraw of its menu work on every pixel of it, and ask about every item again. A redraw now touches only the lit row and its surroundings, the answers about a menu's items are remembered, and moving the pointer redraws only the rows that change.

Since 1.0.38 (if you skipped it):
- **Menus open without the delay on slow graphics hardware.** The pill menus need the picture of the screen behind them (a window is a rectangle, so the round corners show what lies under the menu). It was taken of the whole screen each time a menu chain opened, which took close to a second on some hardware. Each menu level now captures only the part of the screen it covers, just before it opens, so the cost is a small fraction of that and does not grow with the screen's size.

Since 1.0.37 (if you skipped it):
- **The colour setting is called "SnakeTracker color" and shows what you picked.** In Tracker preferences > Windows, the setting that was called "Menu selector color" is now "SnakeTracker color", with a small square beside it that shows the colour you have chosen; it changes as you move the sliders and goes back when you press Revert or Defaults.

Since 1.0.36 (if you skipped it):
- **The window menu of every program is a pill.** The menu that opens on a program in the Deskbar's list (its windows, then Hide all, Show all and Close all) was a pill only for programs whose windows are listed in the Deskbar itself; where a window shows in the menu (Terminal, for one), the menu kept the default dark look. It is a pill in every layout now, including the mini Deskbar, where the list of programs is itself a menu: that list is a lavender pill with the programs' pills in it, and the window menu opens beside it as a pill.
- **The Deskbar's menu joins the leaf block when the Deskbar runs across the screen.** At the top or the bottom of the screen the snake's bar of the leaf menu runs into the leaf's block: the border between them is covered along the bar, the block's edge is open there, and the menu's corner on the bar's side is square.

Since 1.0.35 (if you skipped it):
- **The Deskbar is drawn in the pill look too.** The whole Deskbar is one lavender panel, in every layout (vertical on either side, horizontal at the top or the bottom, expanded or mini). The leaf sits on a block in the selector colour, rounded and shaded like a raised bar and fading gently towards the panel's colour, and the snake runs straight into it from the menu, with no seam. Each program in the list has a lit pill for a header, and its windows sit in a dark rounded box under it; the tray and the clock sit on the lavender with dark text. The window menu of a program (Hide all, Show all, Close all) is a pill as well. The Deskbar's own menu is lifted to line up with the block. Tray icons that draw their own background keep it.

Since 1.0.34 (if you skipped it):
- **Menus are one solid pill per level, with the snake trail running through them.** The Desktop's right-click menu and the Deskbar's menu (the leaf menu) are drawn as a light rounded pill for each level, in a tint of the selector colour with dark text, and the trail is one connected snake from the Deskbar or the Desktop through every submenu, bending with the same radius as the pill's round ends. The trail is shaded like a round bar lit from the top left, so it looks raised. Each submenu opens 16 pixels over its parent, which covers the parent's arrow and keeps the trail unbroken, and lines up with the parent's row. An arrow that the trail passes through is not drawn; arrows on the other side stay. Beside the Deskbar the menu's corner against it is square and the trail runs into the leaf. Menus in other programs, and menu bars, are not changed.

Since 1.0.33 (if you skipped it):
- **No more "Bash is waiting to quit" when you restart or shut down.** The start-up job that hands Tracker and Deskbar over to these builds was a shell script, which the system registers as an application called "Bash" and asks to quit at shutdown, and a script cannot answer. It waited out the system's 3 second limit each time. It is now a small background server, `snaketracker_server` (in `/boot/system/servers`), that starts with the desktop like the other system servers, does the same work (hands Tracker and Deskbar over if the launch daemon would start the system's, and starts them again if one crashes) and answers the system when it asks it to quit. If the launch daemon already starts these builds, it does nothing. The server has an icon of its own: the smiling snake, with human teeth, surfing on a red board with its tail wrapped round a globe.

Since 1.0.32 (if you skipped it):
- **Buttons in Qt programs look right.** In programs written with Qt (Cricket's Qt version, the Qt6 Configurator and the like), the buttons showed vertical stripes across their top half. Qt draws each button on an off-screen picture and puts it on the window with its see-through parts respected, and the control look left parts of the button see-through. The button's gloss, its side caps and the pressed column title are now drawn so that the button stays solid. Nothing changes in programs that draw buttons the usual Haiku way: the buttons look the same there.

Since 1.0.31 (if you skipped it):
- **A side panel in folder windows.** Every folder window has a panel at its left, with a splitter you can drag: your favourite folders and a tree that opens folder by folder. It is on by default; **Window > Side panel** turns it off or on (windows opened later follow your choice). It is not in the Desktop, Trash or query windows, or in Open and Save dialogs. The folder the window shows is kept selected, with the same light pill as selected files, and clicking a folder shows it in the same window.
  - **Favorites.** The panel starts with Home, Desktop and Downloads. Drag a folder from the file view onto the panel to add it, or right-click any folder and choose **Add to favorites**; right-click a favourite for **Remove from favorites**, **Move up**, **Move down** and **Open in new window** (always a window of its own, even where the folder is already open). The list is one file, `~/config/settings/Tracker/SnakeFavorites`, a folder to a line.
  - **Disks.** A mounted disk, a USB stick for instance, shows up under the favourites when it is mounted and goes when it is unmounted, without restarting Tracker. The boot disk, the package volumes (`system`, `config`) and Haiku's virtual file systems are never listed. **Unmount** is in the right-click menu of a disk's top folder only, never for those.
  - **Trash.** A Trash line shows whether the Trash is full or empty and follows it. Clicking it shows the Trash in the window; its right-click menu has **Empty Trash** and **Hide Trash**, and right-clicking the empty space in the panel has **Show Trash** or **Hide Trash**.
- **Icons keep themselves in order.** In icon and mini icon view the icons are put in sorted order on the grid when you choose the view or another icon size, when the window opens or shows another folder, when the window is resized (they run on to fit the new width), and shortly after a file or folder is made, deleted, renamed or moved in or out of the folder, or icons are dropped in the window. It waits until you stop typing a name or dragging, keeps your selection and your place in the window, and leaves list view and the Desktop alone. Your own placements of icons do not stay.
- **Dragging over folders.** The pill that marks the folder under the cursor no longer leaves its ends behind when the cursor moves on.
- **The Deskbar's trail starts halfway up the leaf bar**, where it meets the bar's own colour, and the edge between the menu and the Deskbar is covered from there, so the purple runs through unbroken.

Since 1.0.30 (if you skipped it):
- **Icons of its own.** The Tracker in this package has an icon of its own, a smiling yellow snake coiled up with its head raised, and the Deskbar has one too: the same snake peeking up from behind a wooden desk, in thin round glasses, with a grin and half-open eyes. They are the icons you see for the two programs wherever Haiku shows them (a Tracker window listing `/system/apps/SnakeTracker`, the Deskbar's team list). Both are vector icons and stay sharp at any size. The Deskbar's leaf button and the stock Tracker and Deskbar are unchanged.

Since 1.0.29 (if you skipped it):
- **Selected files are a pill.** In Tracker windows and on the Desktop, the name of a selected file sits on a pill in a light tint of the selector colour, with dark text on it, in place of the stark reversed (white) box. In a window that is not active it is a more muted pill. It works in list, icon and mini icon views, and while you drag a selection. The other columns of the row keep their normal text colour.
- **Renaming a file uses the same pill.** The edit box has no border and no white background: it is the pill's colour with dark text, and the selected text is a deeper tint of the accent instead of an inverted block. The pill follows the box as the name gets longer or shorter, and leaves nothing behind when you stop editing, in windows and on the Desktop. Over a wallpaper nothing is erased behind the pill.
- **The lines between menu items are thin pills.** In Tracker's and the Deskbar's menus (the Be menu, the Desktop and window context menus, Mount, New and the like) the separator is a line about a pixel and a half thick with rounded ends, tinted a little towards the selector colour, and fading out at both ends. It follows light and dark themes, the same on the Desktop as in a window. Menus in other programs keep the stock lines: Haiku draws those itself.
- **Check marks in Tracker's menus** are a smaller copy of the check box's mark: a curved stroke in the selector colour (brighter on a dark menu, darker on a light one) with a soft shadow. On the highlighted row it takes the row's text colour. A check mark in another program's menu stays white.

Since 1.0.28 (if you skipped it):
- **No more tearing behind a dragged window.** Dragging a window over a stationary one left black blocks across the menu bar of the window underneath until you let go. The bar is now drawn whole whatever part of it is uncovered, and it still redraws completely when the window is resized.
- **The start-up job stands down when the system shuts down.** On systems where the launch daemon starts the system's own Tracker and Deskbar first, the package's start-up job takes over and watches the two programs. It now stops when the launch daemon stops it, and its watchers no longer start a program again once it is gone, so nothing of the package is left running or restarting while the system goes down. On systems where the daemon already starts the snake-trail builds the job does nothing and is unchanged.

Since 1.0.27 (if you skipped it):
- **Menu bars are accent pills.** Every window's menu bar, in Tracker and in other programs, is a rounded bar in the selector colour, with the title you open drawn as a darker pill inside it. The bar is drawn whole again when the window is resized, so it leaves no stray rounded ends. The Deskbar's own bar is left alone.
- **The trail runs down the menus of a menu bar.** A menu opened from a menu bar title (File, Window, Attributes) now has the strip down its left edge from the top of the menu to the row you are on, as the Deskbar's menu does. Tracker's own menu rows no longer paint over it.
- **Tracker's toolbar.** Back, forward and up are round, solid discs in the selector colour (washed out when disabled, darker when pressed), with new arrows drawn half as large again. The folder icon at the end of the menu bar sits on a matching disc.
- **Pill tabs.** In a tab view the selected tab is an accent pill and the others are plain labels, with a thin rule between the tabs and the page. Tabs on the left or right keep the old look.
- **Drop-down fields are pills** with a divider and a chevron, in the selector colour.
- **Column list headers in other programs** are one accent bar, like Tracker's.
- **Radio buttons and check boxes.** The radio button is a recessed disc with the accent dot, and check boxes get the same recessed fill. The check mark is one even stroke.
- **Buttons.** Buttons down to 24 pixels wide get the accent caps, three pixels wide on the small ones. Small square buttons (under 34 pixels) are a solid round disc instead.
- **Light or dark follows the window's colour.** Which shade to use for the caps, the default button and the marks is now decided from the panel colour, not from the button's own, so a panel in the middle of the range (around 104 to 127) no longer gets Haiku's bright ring around the default button. The faint line round every button is drawn in the window's colour, and the see-through corners of the pills sit on the window's colour too.

Since 1.0.26 (if you skipped it):
- **Buttons follow the theme.** Every regular button has an accent cap on each side, now a five pixel block lit from the top with a darker line on its outer edge: a darker purple on a light theme and a lighter one on a dark theme. The disabled buttons have the same caps.
- **The default button.** On a light theme it has a dark accent body with white text. On a dark theme it is drawn like the other buttons, without Haiku's bright two pixel ring (it is still the button Enter presses), in the room the ring used to take, so it stays the same size.
- **The Deskbar's top bar is left alone.** The leaf bar and its title keep the stock look, without caps. The snake trail in the Deskbar's menus is as before.
- **File panels in other programs.** The column titles and the item count of a Save or Open dialog, which the Tracker kit draws inside each program, are the same accent bars as in Tracker. Outside Tracker the text colour is the system's, so the bar takes a deeper shade of the accent behind light text and a lighter one behind dark text. The thin lines between the column titles stay there. The bars are drawn whole again when the dialog is resized.

Since 1.0.25 (if you skipped it):
- **Scroll bar arrows are hidden by default.** The arrow buttons at the ends of scroll bars are now drawn as plain track, so scroll bars are just the thumb pill on a flat track. This is a new setting, **Tracker preferences > Windows > Scroll bar arrows**, and it is off by default: tick it to bring the arrows back. The bar keeps the room the arrows had, so the thumb stops a little short of each end, and the hidden arrows still scroll when you click them. **If you are updating, your scroll bar arrows disappear until you tick the box.**
- **The column titles and the item count are an accent bar.** In a Tracker list view the Name, Size and Modified header is one full width bar in the selector colour, with rounded ends and the same lit look as the scroll bar thumb. The "27 items" bar at the bottom matches it. The text is dark or light to suit the colour, the lines between the titles are gone, and the sort arrow takes the text colour. A title being pressed darkens its part of the bar. Both bars are drawn whole again when the window is resized, so they leave nothing behind. When you type to jump to a file, the bottom bar shows what you typed in the stock look.
- **Sliders.** The thumb is a rounded block in the selector colour, lit like the scroll bar thumb, and disabled sliders get a washed out one. It keeps the size Haiku gives it. The bar to the left of the thumb is filled in the selector colour, a washed out one when disabled.
- **Check boxes.** Both checked and unchecked boxes have a recessed fill, a shade darker than the panel (lighter on a dark one) with a touch of the selector colour. The tick is the selector colour: dark on a light panel and brighter on a dark one. In 1.0.24 it came out pale on dark desktops.
- **Buttons.** Every regular button has an accent cap on each side: a dark outer bar and a lighter band that fades into the button. The disabled buttons have them too. The small buttons (scroll bar arrows, the + and - next to a size) are left as they were.

Since 1.0.24 (if you skipped it):

- **Sliders and progress bars.** A slider is filled in the selector colour up to its thumb, and progress bars are filled in it as well. Programs that choose their own fill colour keep it.

Since 1.0.23 (if you skipped it):
- **The scroll bar thumb sits on the track without a box around it.** The thumb was drawn on a patch of a slightly different grey from the track beside it, which showed as a lighter rectangle around the pill. The patch now uses the same colour as the track.

Since 1.0.22 (if you skipped it):
- **Scroll bars with nothing to scroll no longer fall back to the stock look.** When a window's content fits, its scroll bar now gets the same flat track as the others, with no thumb, so it reads as an empty trough. The arrow buttons keep their stock disabled look.

Since 1.0.21 (if you skipped it):
- **The snake trail starts at the Deskbar.** The Deskbar's own menu (the leaf menu) now carries the trail from the Deskbar to the row you are on. When the menu opens beside the Deskbar (vertical, expanded), a strip runs down the edge facing the Deskbar from the top of the menu to your row, and the window border between the two is covered so the strip runs into the Deskbar. When it opens below the leaf (a top bar, or a vertical bar that is not expanded) the strip runs down the side the leaf is on, from the top of the menu. When it opens above the leaf (a bottom bar) it runs from your row to the bottom of the menu.
- **Scroll bars match the selector.** The scroll bar thumb is a rounded pill in the selector colour, lighter down its middle, on a flat track (SnakeControlLook only). In a window that is not active the pill stays, washed out towards grey. The colour is the same one the menus use, so it follows hDesktop's Selector Color and not the window decorator's theme.

Since 1.0.20 (if you skipped it):
- **The vertical part of the trail bulges too.** Where a submenu's selected row is not level with the row that opened it, the strip joining the two now hangs a few pixels into the parent menu, rounded at its free end and outlined on its inner side, so the whole trail reads as one shape. Where the submenu's row is lower (or higher) than the parent menu reaches, the strip carries on past the parent's edge over the desktop, so it no longer disappears as you move down a long menu. When the rows are level there is no vertical part and nothing changes.

Since 1.0.17 (if you skipped it):
- **The selected row bulges out of the menu.** The row you are on sticks out a few pixels past the menu's outer edge, as a small outlined tab. It is on the edge away from the neighbouring menu, so it flips sides when a submenu opens on the other side of the screen.
- **Dark outline.** The selector has a one pixel dark outline that follows the whole trail.
- **Flat selector.** The selector is a single flat colour inside the outline. The light top edge and dark bottom edge are still available: turn off **Tracker preferences > Windows > Flat menu selector** to bring them back. The setting applies to Tracker and Deskbar menus, to every program's menus when SnakeControlLook is the control look, to menu bar titles and to the seam between submenu windows. hDesktop can override it with its `nav_snake_flat` setting while it runs.

## Install
Two packages are attached: `snaketracker-1.0.43-1-x86_64.hpkg` for 64-bit Haiku and `snaketracker-1.0.43-1-x86_gcc2.hpkg` for 32-bit Haiku (gcc 2 / hybrid). Download the one for your system and install it **for the whole system**, not for "home". A home install is silently ignored, because the file that tells Haiku to start the new Tracker and Deskbar is only read from the system location.
